tokens and networks.

Choose ETH, ARB, USDC or USDT on Arbitrum One for a same-network token swap, or select a destination on another chain. The cross-chain selector also includes ETH, USDC and USDT on Ethereum, BTC on Bitcoin, SOL on Solana and BNB on BNB Chain.

Arbitrum Swap FAQ

How is Arbitrum Swap different from a bridge?
A same-network swap changes one token into another on Arbitrum. A bridge moves value between networks. A cross-chain swap combines a network change with a token exchange, such as ETH on Arbitrum to USDC on Ethereum.
Which tokens and networks can I select?
The interface includes ETH, ARB, USDC and USDT on Arbitrum; ETH, USDC and USDT on Ethereum; and native BTC, SOL and BNB on Bitcoin, Solana and BNB Chain. A selectable pair is not a confirmed route: availability and minimum amounts require a live quote.
What fees apply to an Arbitrum Swap?
Compare the final receive amount, route or provider fee, and network costs before funding an order. Sending tokens from Arbitrum requires ETH for gas. Fees depend on the pair and route; this interface does not currently provide live fee quotes.
Who holds my funds during the exchange?
You control funds until you send them. A deposit-based exchange then uses the selected provider or route contracts to process the transfer before delivery to your receiving address. Keeping your private keys does not mean funds remain in your custody throughout settlement. Review provider and refund terms before sending.
Do I need to connect a wallet, register or complete KYC?
No wallet connection or registration is needed to use this interface. The intended deposit-based flow uses a receiving address and a transfer from your own wallet. Identity checks depend on the execution provider and its rules; a universal no-KYC guarantee cannot be confirmed without a live route.
How quickly does a swap finish, and how do points work?
Settlement depends on network confirmations, liquidity and provider processing. Points are intended for completed exchanges, with eligibility and amounts shown alongside an executable quote. Live swaps and points accrual are currently unavailable; selecting a pair does not create an order.
