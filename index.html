Arbitrum Swap.

An Arbitrum Swap trades ETH, USDC and other tokens on Arbitrum, an Ethereum layer-2 where fees are a fraction of mainnet. A DEX or aggregator routes your order across liquidity pools and shows the minimum received before you sign, your gas is paid in ETH, and your funds stay in your own wallet the whole time.

How to make an Arbitrum Swap

Connect a wallet, choose the pair, and confirm. To make an Arbitrum Swap you pick the tokens, review the live rate, the minimum received at your slippage and the network fee, then approve the token once and sign. The trade settles on Arbitrum only after you sign, and fees are low because it is a layer-2.

Arbitrum Swap fees and rate

An Arbitrum Swap costs the live market rate plus a small L2 gas fee and the pool spread; there is no custody and no account. Price impact grows with trade size, so the quote shows the minimum received as the floor at your chosen slippage. Small trades cost cents compared with Ethereum mainnet.

Which tokens can you swap on Arbitrum?

You can swap ETH, USDC, USDT, ARB and other liquid tokens through Arbitrum DEXs and aggregators. The reachable list depends on routing liquidity at the moment; the interface shows the expected output for each pair so you can compare before trading.

Is an Arbitrum Swap safe?

A self-custody swap keeps your keys in your wallet. Confirm the destination token's contract address, the amount received and the token approval you grant, prefer a limited approval over an unlimited one, and treat any request for a seed phrase as fraud.

Swapping on Arbitrum vs on Ethereum mainnet

The same pair usually costs far less on Arbitrum than on Ethereum mainnet because gas is a fraction of the base layer. If your assets are already on Arbitrum, swapping there is cheapest; otherwise weigh the bridge cost against the fees saved, judging by the net amount received.
Do I need ETH for an Arbitrum Swap?
Yes. Gas on Arbitrum is paid in ETH, so keep a small amount even when the tokens you trade are stablecoins.
Why is an Arbitrum Swap cheaper than on Ethereum?
Arbitrum is a layer-2 rollup, so gas is a fraction of Ethereum mainnet while still settling to it.
Do I need an account to swap on Arbitrum?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and trade on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody of your funds.
Why do I receive less than the quote?
Price impact and slippage on your size, plus the gas and pool fees, reduce the output. The minimum received is the guaranteed floor at your slippage.
What happens if my swap fails?
A failed transaction reverts and you keep your tokens, though the gas for the attempt is spent.
